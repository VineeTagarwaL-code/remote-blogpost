I’ve always loved traveling. For a long time, though, it felt out of reach. Life had its own constraints, and the idea of exploring new places kept getting postponed.

That’s why looking back at this year feels surreal.

Despite how it began, I ended up traveling to **25 different places**—something I’m deeply grateful for and something I never imagined would happen when the year first started.

These are the places that i travelled to:

* Vaishno Devi
* Manali
* Solang
* Pune
* Lonavala
* Mumbai
* Tatanagar
* Bangalore
* Darjeeling
* Gangtok
* Lachung
* Zero Point
* Siliguri
* Mandarmani
* Bodh Gaya
* Varanasi
* Haridwar
* Lansdowne
* Rishikesh
* Devprayag
* Amritsar
* Chandigarh
* Dalhousie
* Delhi
* Pakistan Border

At the start of the year, none of this felt possible.

I was in a very low place mentally. Without going too deep into details, I was deeply unhappy with where I was in life. Every day felt heavy. Waking up itself felt like a struggle, and there were moments when it genuinely felt like I was slipping into a grave depression.

Life was hard—plain and simple.

But even during that phase, there was one thing I held onto: the belief that this was temporary. I didn’t know how things would change, or when, but I knew I had to keep going. Giving up was never an option, even when everything felt overwhelming.

The turning point came unexpectedly.

Yet, somewhere beneath all that weight, there was a quiet belief I held onto—that this phase was temporary. Even when things felt unbearable, I kept reminding myself that I wouldn’t be here forever. I didn’t have clarity, but I had patience. And that was enough to keep me going.

Slowly, things began to shift.

Not suddenly. Not dramatically. Just quietly.

As days passed, life started to feel lighter. Movement replaced stagnation. New places brought perspective. Long journeys gave me time to think, to breathe, to heal. Traveling didn’t fix everything—but it gave me space, and sometimes that’s all you need.

This year taught me that growth doesn’t always come from big wins. Sometimes it comes from surviving difficult days, trusting time, and allowing yourself to move forward even when you don’t feel ready.

I’m grateful—not because the year was easy, but because it reminded me that even the hardest moments eventually give way to change.

Life has a way of teaching you.
You just have to stay long enough to listen.
